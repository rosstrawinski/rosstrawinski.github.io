I got into engineering through building things that had to actually fly — quadcopters that had to survive a crash, structures that had to hold up under load. That hands-on start is still how I like to work: design it, build it, test it, and fix what breaks.

At UMD I'm part of the Terrapin Rockets Aerostructures team, laying up and winding composite airframe components toward an IREC competition launch. Outside of coursework, I design and fly FPV quadcopters and have led a subteam building an autonomous ground vehicle.
